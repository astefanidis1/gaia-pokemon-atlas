GAIA PUBLIC METHODOLOGY BRIEF

How GAIA knows what is alive

Population, position, and public precision are separate measurements. A precise census does not imply that every refuge is publicly disclosed.

01 · Persistent signature

Every independently living Pokémon produces a stable bioenergetic signature. Eggs enter the census only after hatching; temporary projections and detached cells are reconciled to their originating organism.

02 · Sensor reconciliation

Orbital aura-spectrum sensors, Pokédex networks, registered Poké Ball telemetry, ecological surveys, terrestrial stations, and dimensional detectors are compared continuously.

03 · Population audit

Conflicting readings are reviewed against movement histories, known forms, breeding records, containment logs, and anomaly signatures before a living total is certified.

04 · Public disclosure

GAIA may publish an exact living total while reducing map precision for nests, refuges, vulnerable colonies, containment facilities, and high-risk individuals.
